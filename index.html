Game Center'ı kullanmayı seçerseniz Apple, Game Center oturumu, oyuncu kimliği/takma adı, liderlik tabloları ve gönderilen skorlar gibi bilgileri kendi hizmeti kapsamında işleyebilir. Game Center verilerinizi yönetmek için Apple cihazınızdaki Game Center ayarlarını ve Apple'ın ilgili destek kaynaklarını kullanın.

İsteğe bağlı hatırlatmalar, iOS'un yerel bildirim sistemiyle cihazınızda planlanır. Bildirim izni vermek zorunda değilsiniz; izni iOS Ayarları'ndan değiştirebilirsiniz. Uygulama, bu hatırlatmaları planlamak için kendi sunucusuna ihtiyaç duymaz.

Yerel oyun verileri siz uygulama içindeki sıfırlama seçeneğini kullanana, uygulama verilerini kaldırana veya ilgili iOS işlevleriyle silinene kadar cihazda kalabilir.

Neon Serpent — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

1. Overview

Neon Serpent is a snake game that primarily stores gameplay progress and preferences on your device. This policy is based on the reviewed source code for this version, which does not contain a developer-hosted account system, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, or code that sends data to a developer-operated server.

2. Information stored on your device

Game scores, high scores, statistics, achievement progress, streak information, selected theme and language, gameplay preferences, and notification settings may be stored locally to operate the app. The app does not send this locally stored information to a developer-operated server. Deleting the app may remove local app data according to iOS behavior.

3. Game Center

If you choose to use Game Center, Apple may process information such as your Game Center session, player identifier or nickname, leaderboard participation, and submitted scores as part of its service. Apple's terms and privacy practices apply. Game Center is optional. Manage Game Center information through Apple's available account and device controls.

4. Notifications

Optional reminders are scheduled locally through iOS notifications. You do not have to grant notification permission, and you can change it in iOS Settings. The app does not need its own server to schedule these reminders.

5. Sharing

If you use the score or game-sharing feature, the iOS share sheet may appear. Any service you select may process information you choose to share under its own privacy policy. Sharing is optional.

6. Tracking, advertising, and analytics

No advertising network or third-party analytics SDK was identified in the reviewed source code for this version, and the app is not designed to track users across apps for advertising. If advertising, analytics, or other third-party SDKs are added in the future, this policy and the App Store Connect privacy disclosures will be updated before release.

7. Sharing and retention

The developer does not upload locally stored gameplay data to its own servers or sell it. Apple services such as Game Center and services you select through the iOS share sheet may process data separately as part of their features. Local gameplay data may remain on the device until reset in the app, removed with app data, or deleted using relevant iOS features.

8. Deletion and choices

  • Gameplay statistics and records can be reset using “Reset statistics and records” in the app.
  • Notification permission can be changed in iOS Settings > Notifications.
  • Game Center data should be managed through Apple's Game Center controls.
  • This version does not provide a developer-operated user account or a user profile stored on a developer server.

9. Children's privacy

The app is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. The intended audience and age rating must be declared accurately in App Store Connect.

10. Security

When information is stored on your device, your device passcode, operating-system security, and backup settings matter. No electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Changes

This policy may be updated if app features or data practices change. The current version will be published on this page.
